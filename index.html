How to use the tracker

Getting started

Click Add, search for a champion, and set their stars, rank, level, awakened and signature, and ascension. Your roster saves automatically in this browser.

Keep your data safe

Your roster is saved only in this browser, on this device. To back it up or move it, open Settings → Save to: File, then Save JSON to download it or Load JSON to restore it. On Chrome and Edge you can connect a folder to save automatically.

Views and filtering

The tabs switch between Roster and Prestige; bosses live under Settings → View unplayable. Filters narrows by stars, class, tag, rank, awakened, and ascension; Sort reorders; card size is bottom-right.

Tags

Add your own tags (like AW Defense or Arena) when editing a champion, then filter by them. Manage or delete them in the Filters panel.

Editing and removing

Click Select, then pick champions. Choose one to Edit it, or select several to Delete them together.

Prestige

Your champion prestige is the average of your top 30 7★ champions (relics aren't counted). Recommended next rank-ups lists which single rank-up raises it the most — work down the list. Full reference shows every 7★, with the ones you own highlighted at their current rank.

Tip: use Save JSON to back up before big changes, and Load JSON to restore if anything looks wrong.
